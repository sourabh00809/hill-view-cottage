About

A quiet base in Garudadri Nagar

Hill View Cottage is a cottage-style hotel & lodge on Bhanumati Road, beside Sannidhanam Guest House - one of Tirumala's most convenient addresses for pilgrims.

Built for short, restful stays: clean rooms, hot water, easy check-in, and hosts who understand early-morning darshan timings. No noise, no hassle - just a calm hillside corner to return to.

FAQ

Good to know before you book

What are check-in / check-out times?

Standard check-in 12 PM, check-out 11 AM. Early check-in / late check-out on request - message on WhatsApp with your arrival time.

How do I book?

Tap WhatsApp or Call, share dates & guests, and the host sends confirmation. Pay at property or via UPI - no online payment gateway needed.

Is parking available?

Two-wheeler parking at the property. For cars, nearby paid parking; autos and taxis can drop you at the gate.

Food nearby?

Many veg eateries and tiffin centres within 300 m. Host can suggest the nearest options and temple prasadam timings.

Cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 24 hrs before check-in. Same-day cancellations may be chargeable in peak season.

Is this the official HVC site?

Demo site built as a portfolio project. For official booking, verify phone on Google Maps listing (CID 8134190655803957827).
